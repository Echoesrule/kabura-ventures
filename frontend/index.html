How It Works

Four steps, no fluff. Here's how to go from dreaming to driving through the savannah.

1. Pick Your Safari

Browse real itineraries with live availability. Every tour shows what's included, where you'll sleep, and what wildlife you're likely to spot each day.

2. Send an Inquiry

Tell us your dates, group size, and any quirks. A real human on our team reads every inquiry and gets back to you within 24 hours — no chatbots here.

3. Lock It In

Once you're happy, we send a detailed confirmation, handle the deposit, take care of park fees, transfers, and permits. You just pack.

4. Live the Adventure

Show up and soak it in. Wake to the sound of hyenas calling across the Mara, sip chai by the campfire, watch a million flamingos lift off Lake Nakuru at dusk.
