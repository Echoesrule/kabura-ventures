Discover Thrilling Locations

Maasai Mara
Narok County, Kenya

The crown jewel of African wildlife reserves. Every year between July and October, over two million wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles surge across its grasslands in the Great Migration — the greatest wildlife spectacle on Earth.

Mount Kenya
Central Kenya

Africa's second-highest peak and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, Mount Kenya rises dramatically from equatorial forests to glacial cirques. The trek to Point Lenana at 4,985 metres passes through bamboo jungles, giant heather moorlands, and otherworldly lobelia forests.
