General

How do I create an account?

Click the user icon in the top-right corner and select "Sign Up". Fill in your name, email, and password. Once registered, you can browse tours, hotels, save items to your wishlist, and make bookings.

How do I reset my password?

On the login page, click "Forgot Password" and enter your registered email address. We'll send you a link to reset your password. If you don't receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data. Your payment information is processed securely through our trusted partners and is never stored on our servers.

How does the currency switcher work?

You can switch between KES (Kenyan Shillings), USD (US Dollars), and EUR (Euros) using the dropdown in the navigation bar. Prices will automatically convert using live exchange rates.

Bookings

How do I book a tour or hotel?

Browse our tours or hotels and click "Details" or "View" on any listing. On the detail page, select your preferred date, number of guests, and click "Book Now". Follow the checkout process to confirm your reservation.

Can I modify or cancel a booking?

Yes. Go to "My Bookings" in your account menu to view all your reservations. Depending on the cancellation policy of the specific tour or hotel, you may be able to modify or cancel your booking. Please refer to the terms shown during checkout.

How will I know my booking is confirmed?

After completing your booking, you'll receive a confirmation email with your booking reference number. You can also view all your confirmed bookings under "My Bookings" in your account.

Can I book for a group?

Absolutely! During the booking process, you can specify the number of guests. For large groups or corporate retreats, visit our Group & Corporate Travel page or contact us directly for customized packages.

Payments

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and M-Pesa (Kenya's mobile money service). All payments are processed securely through trusted payment gateways.

Do you require a deposit?

Deposit requirements vary by tour or hotel. Most bookings require a 30-50% deposit at the time of booking, with the balance due before or upon arrival. Specific terms are displayed during checkout.

What is your refund policy?

Refund policies vary by provider:

  • Tours: Free cancellation up to 48 hours before departure. 50% refund for cancellations within 24-48 hours.
  • Hotels: Free cancellation up to 24 hours before check-in.

Please refer to the specific terms shown during booking.

Tours & Safaris

What should I pack for a safari?

We recommend packing light, neutral-colored clothing, comfortable walking shoes, a hat, sunscreen, insect repellent, binoculars, a camera, and a reusable water bottle. For specific tours, a detailed packing list is provided in your booking confirmation.

Are meals included in tour packages?

Meal inclusions vary by tour. Most full-day and multi-day tours include breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Check the specific tour details on the listing page for exact inclusions.

What is the maximum group size?

Group sizes vary by tour type. Our standard safari tours typically accommodate 6-8 guests per vehicle for an intimate experience. Private tours are also available for solo travelers, couples, or groups who prefer a personalized experience.

Are children allowed on tours?

Yes, children are welcome on most tours. Age restrictions may apply for certain adventure activities. Check the specific tour listing for age requirements or contact us for family-friendly recommendations.

Hotels & Accommodation

What types of accommodation do you offer?

We offer a wide range of accommodation options including luxury lodges, boutique hotels, budget-friendly guesthouses, beach resorts, and tented camps. Use the filters on our Hotels page to find the perfect stay for your budget and preferences.

Can I request early check-in or late check-out?

Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability. You can request these during the booking process or by contacting the property directly. Additional charges may apply.

Are airport transfers included?

Airport transfers are not automatically included in hotel bookings. However, you can add airport transfers through our Airport Transports service or request them when making your reservation.
